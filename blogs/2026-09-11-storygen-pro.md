StoryGen Pro September 11, 2026 StoryGen Pro converts Pega GenAI Blueprints, BRDs, meeting transcripts, or plain-text requirements into structured, delivery-ready Agile user stories, directly inside Agile Studio. It automatically derives acceptance criteria and exception scenarios stage-by-stage, using the Blueprint as the single source of truth. Built for product owners, business analysts, scrum masters, and delivery teams, it supports both new Blueprint-driven journeys and existing Pega case types.
